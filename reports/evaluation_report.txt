======================================================================
SECURITY METRIC DISCUSSION: RECALL VS PRECISION TRADE-OFF
======================================================================
In phishing detection, Recall is of critical importance because a False Negative
(failing to flag a phishing URL) exposes users directly to credential theft, fraud,
or malware infection. Conversely, a False Positive disrupts user workflow with a false alarm.
Therefore, models are evaluated primarily on F1-Score to balance both, ensuring high
threat detection while maintaining acceptable precision.
